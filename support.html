Frequently asked questions

About the app

What is Novelist?
Novelist is an AI-assisted story-writing app. Pick a genre, tone, point of view, and other options, add characters, and Novelist writes chapters with you one at a time — you can regenerate a chapter you don't like, redirect the plot, or fine-tune it with your own instructions. It also includes Discover, a bundled library of ready-to-read stories across genres.

Is the app free?
Yes. Novelist is free to download, and starting a story and writing a number of chapters is free. Once you reach the free limit, an optional Novelist Pro subscription (weekly, monthly, or yearly) removes the limit.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone or iPad: open Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Novelist → Cancel Subscription. Apple handles all subscription billing — we can't cancel on your behalf.

How do I restore a previous purchase?
Open the paywall (Settings → Novelist Pro, or the prompt shown when you hit a free-tier limit) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed into the App Store with the Apple ID you used to subscribe.

I was charged but the app still shows locked content.
Tap Restore Purchases in the paywall first — that resolves almost all cases. If it's still locked, email us and we'll help.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Novelist purchase, and request a refund.

Using the app

How do I start a new story?
Tap Generate, pick a genre and your other options (tone, point of view, ending, pacing, audience, writing style), optionally add characters, and Novelist writes the opening chapter.

How do I regenerate a chapter or redirect the plot?
Open a chapter and use the toolbar's Regenerate or Redirect Plot options. Each story has a limited number of regenerations to keep things fair for everyone.

What does Fine-Tune do?
Fine-Tune lets you type your own instructions to steer the next chapter — a character's motivation, a tone shift, a specific event you want to happen next.

How do I listen to a story?
Tap the Listen button in the reader. This uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice — no internet connection or account is required for narration.

How do I export or share a story?
Open a story's menu and choose Export — EPUB, PDF, or plain text — then share it however you like using the iOS share sheet.

What is Discover?
Discover is a built-in library of ready-to-read stories across genres, bundled with the app — no generation or subscription required to read them.

Privacy and data

What data does the app collect?
Your stories and characters stay on your device. We don't ask for a name, email, or login — just an anonymous account used to track your subscription and free-tier usage. When you generate a chapter, the story details you provide are sent to our backend and forwarded to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to write the text. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
